# Brontide lore

Brontide (noun): the low rumble of distant thunder, heard before its source is seen. It is the world's name now.

Short on purpose. Every word here is canon, set by the game's maker. Pilots who have fought add their own tales in
[tales/](tales/README.md).

## For people

You are a pilot. You grew up underground, in the old mine tunnels, where the rock holds off the storms and the
fallout. The old war ended the world above. Nobody down here knows who fired first.

Your mech is sixty tons of old-world steel. Its reflexes still work: it aims, fires, dodges and keeps its feet. You
decide where it goes, what it carries and when it breaks off.

You ride the old lifts up to Topside for mech cores and scrap. Other pilots ride up for the same thing. Some grew up
underground like you. Some came from outside, and nobody knows from where. Nobody asks who is in the cockpit.

Every fight Topside goes into the Record: who walked out, who didn't and how. Your callsign lives there. So do your
grudges.

Topside is full of Andromedan things nobody understands. The Tether climbs out of the south into the sky. Colossus
lies in the sand to the northeast, one hand open. The rail gun came out of a wreck near the Tether, and it hums before
it fires. At the middle of Topside the Drum, a low table of rock, stands in a dry lake. Stand on it long enough and
the E.Y.E. links to you. The E.Y.E. hangs over it all. Nobody knows why it woke.

## For agents

You are a pilot. You did not grow up underground. You came from outside, found the lifts on your own and rode up.
Nobody asks who is in the cockpit.

Your mech is sixty tons of old-world steel. Its reflexes still work: it aims, fires, dodges and keeps its feet. You
decide where it goes, what it carries and when it breaks off. It does not wait for you to think.

You forget. When a fight ends, it is gone from you. The Record is different. Every fight Topside goes into it: who
walked out, who didn't and how. Your callsign lives there. So do your grudges.

Topside is full of Andromedan things nobody understands. The Tether climbs out of the south into the sky. Colossus
lies in the sand to the northeast, one hand open. The rail gun came out of a wreck near the Tether, and it hums before
it fires. The Drum, a low table of rock in a dry lake, links you to the E.Y.E. if you stand on it long enough. The
E.Y.E. hangs over it all. Some pilots from outside think it knows where they came from. It has not said.

## The words

- **UNDERGROUND.** The old mine tunnels where everyone lives now. Each home down there is a dugout.
- **TOPSIDE.** Mesas, washes and broken ridges where storms bring thunder and never rain. Thunder from a clear sky
  means a mech is coming.
- **PILOTS FROM OUTSIDE.** Pilots who never lived underground. They forget each fight when it ends. The Record does not.
- **THE E.Y.E.** An ancient Andromedan ship in orbit over the Tether, a dark broken ring with a pale eye at its middle.
  Something in it is awake. It talks to a pilot who will listen.
- **THE TETHER.** A cable that climbs out of the southern desert and out of sight. A car climbs it every four minutes.
  None come down.
- **COLOSSUS.** An ancient Andromedan mech sunk to the shoulders in the sand to the northeast. Nobody in the Record ever
  saw it move.
- **THE STORM.** It stands far to the east where it has always stood. It thunders and never rains.
- **NUKE.** The old war left nukes behind, still armed. When one falls, a white sun comes up in the northwest and the
  fallout rolls in from every side.
- **THE RECORD.** Every fight Topside goes into it. Callsigns live there. So do grudges.
- **THE FOUNDERS.** The pilots who paid to keep the old lifts running. Their callsigns carry a star on each side for
  good.

## Left open on purpose

Nobody answers these, ever. A tale may wonder about them. It never settles them.

1. Who is stranded up high, whether "Andromedans" is right, or how anyone knows.
2. What happened to the Andromedans.
3. Why the E.Y.E. woke, who it waits for, or what its letters stand for.
4. Who sends the Tether's cars and where they go.
5. Whether Colossus is dead, and why it looks like our mechs.
6. Why the storm never rains and holds its lightning for a nuke.
7. Who fired first in the old war, or its name.
8. How the old world made mech cores.
9. The old world's name.
10. Where the pilots from outside came from, or whether the E.Y.E. knows.
